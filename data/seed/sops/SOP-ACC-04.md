# [SOP-ACC-04] Shared drive access requests - approval and entitlement check

**Category:** platform_support_access
**Applies to:** shared drive access

## Symptom

I have joined the reporting team this week and need read/write access to the FIN-Reporting share to pick up the month-end pack.

## Root cause

Access granted after manager approval and entitlement review.

## Resolution steps

1. Verified the request against the joiner record and the approving manager.
2. Confirmed the role mapping for the requested share in the entitlement matrix.
3. Raised the grant through the access management workflow for sign-off.
4. Added the user to the correct security group once sign-off was recorded.

## Before you close

- Confirm the outcome with the requester, in their words, not yours.
- If the requester's tier is Gold, hand the close to the named owner for that
  business unit rather than closing directly.
