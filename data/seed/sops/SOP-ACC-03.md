# [SOP-ACC-03] Account lockout and expired password recovery

**Category:** platform_support_access
**Applies to:** password expiry

## Symptom

I was on leave for three weeks and my password expired while I was away. I cannot log in to my workstation to change it.

## Root cause

Account unlocked and password reset through verified self-service path.

## Resolution steps

1. Verified identity against the callback number on the HR record.
2. Unlocked the account in the directory.
3. Walked the user through a self-service reset so the new password is not known to IT.
4. Confirmed successful sign-in before closing.

## Before you close

- Confirm the outcome with the requester, in their words, not yours.
- If the requester's tier is Gold, hand the close to the named owner for that
  business unit rather than closing directly.
